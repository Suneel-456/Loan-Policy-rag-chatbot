SAMPLE BANK - GENERAL LOAN TERMS, SERVICING AND GRIEVANCE POLICY
Version 1.0 | Sample document for a RAG project demo. Sample Bank is a fictional institution.

Section 1. KYC and Onboarding
1.1 PAN and Aadhaar are mandatory for all borrowers and co-applicants.
1.2 If the address on Aadhaar differs from the current address, a separate address proof such as a utility bill or rent agreement is required.
1.3 Video KYC is available for applicants who cannot visit a branch.

Section 2. Loan Processing Timeline
2.1 After the application is submitted, document verification takes up to 2 working days.
2.2 Credit appraisal takes up to 3 working days after verification.
2.3 A sanction letter is issued after approval. The sanction letter is valid for 30 days from the date of issue.
2.4 The loan is disbursed within 2 working days after the loan agreement is signed and the repayment mandate is set up.
2.5 A Key Fact Statement (KFS) showing the interest rate, fees, charges and the total cost of the loan is given to the borrower before the loan agreement is signed.

Section 3. Overdue Classification
3.1 An account with an overdue period of 1 to 30 days is classified as SMA-0.
3.2 An account with an overdue period of 31 to 60 days is classified as SMA-1.
3.3 An account with an overdue period of 61 to 90 days is classified as SMA-2.
3.4 An account with an overdue period of more than 90 days is classified as a Non-Performing Asset (NPA).
3.5 Overdue accounts are reported to credit bureaus, which lowers the borrower's credit score.

Section 4. Collections and Recovery
4.1 Reminders are sent by SMS and email before the EMI due date.
4.2 Collection calls are made only between 8:00 AM and 7:00 PM.
4.3 The bank does not use abusive language or harassment in collection activity.

Section 5. Grievance Redressal
5.1 Level 1: The borrower can raise a complaint at the branch or with customer care. The bank responds within 7 working days.
5.2 Level 2: If the complaint is not resolved, the borrower can escalate to the Nodal Officer. The Nodal Officer responds within 7 working days.
5.3 Level 3: If the complaint is still not resolved, the borrower can escalate to the Principal Nodal Officer.
5.4 If the complaint is not resolved within 30 days of the date it was first raised, the borrower can approach the RBI Integrated Ombudsman.

Section 6. Products Covered
6.1 This document set covers personal loans and home loans only.
6.2 Gold loans, vehicle loans and business loans are governed by separate policies that are not part of this document set.
