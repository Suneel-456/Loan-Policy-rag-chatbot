SAMPLE BANK - PERSONAL LOAN POLICY
Version 1.0 | Sample document for a RAG project demo. Sample Bank is a fictional institution.

Section 1. Eligibility
1.1 Salaried applicants must be between 21 and 58 years of age at the time of application.
1.2 Salaried applicants must have a minimum net monthly income of Rs. 25,000.
1.3 Salaried applicants must have at least 1 year of total work experience, including at least 6 months with the current employer.
1.4 Self-employed applicants must have a minimum business vintage of 2 years and must submit Income Tax Returns for the last 2 years.
1.5 The minimum credit score required is 700. Applications with a credit score below 700 are declined.

Section 2. Loan Amount and Tenure
2.1 The minimum loan amount is Rs. 50,000 and the maximum loan amount is Rs. 25,00,000.
2.2 The loan tenure ranges from 12 months to 60 months.
2.3 The total EMI obligations of the applicant, including the new loan, must not exceed 50% of the net monthly income.

Section 3. Interest Rate and Fees
3.1 Personal loans carry a fixed interest rate. The rate depends on the credit score of the applicant.
3.2 Credit score of 780 and above: 10.5% to 11.5% per annum.
3.3 Credit score of 700 to 779: 11.5% to 15% per annum.
3.4 A processing fee of 1.5% of the sanctioned loan amount plus GST is charged at the time of disbursal.
3.5 Stamp duty is charged as applicable in the state of the borrower.

Section 4. Documents Required
4.1 Identity and address proof: PAN card and Aadhaar for all applicants.
4.2 Income proof for salaried applicants: salary slips for the last 3 months and bank statements for the last 6 months.
4.3 Income proof for self-employed applicants: Income Tax Returns with computation of income for the last 2 years and bank statements for the last 12 months.
4.4 Employment proof for salaried applicants: employee ID card or offer letter.

Section 5. Repayment, Late Payment and EMI Bounce
5.1 The EMI is auto-debited on the due date through a NACH mandate.
5.2 An EMI bounce charge of Rs. 500 plus GST is levied for each bounced instalment.
5.3 Penal charges of 2% per month are levied on the overdue amount for delayed payment.
5.4 Repeated EMI bounces and delayed payments are reported to credit bureaus and affect the borrower's credit score.

Section 6. Prepayment and Foreclosure
6.1 Part prepayment is allowed after 6 EMIs have been paid. The minimum part prepayment amount is equal to 3 EMIs.
6.2 Foreclosure within the first 12 months of the loan attracts a charge of 4% of the outstanding principal.
6.3 Foreclosure after 12 months from disbursal attracts a charge of 2% of the outstanding principal.
