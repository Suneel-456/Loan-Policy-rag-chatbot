SAMPLE BANK - HOME LOAN POLICY
Version 1.0 | Sample document for a RAG project demo. Sample Bank is a fictional institution.

Section 1. Eligibility
1.1 The applicant must be at least 21 years old. The age at loan maturity must not exceed 65 years for salaried applicants and 70 years for self-employed applicants.
1.2 Salaried applicants must have a minimum net monthly income of Rs. 30,000.
1.3 Self-employed applicants must submit Income Tax Returns for the last 3 years.
1.4 The minimum credit score required for a home loan is 725.
1.5 A co-applicant such as a spouse or parent is allowed, and the income of the co-applicant can be combined with the income of the main applicant.

Section 2. Loan Amount, Tenure and Loan to Value (LTV)
2.1 The minimum home loan amount is Rs. 5,00,000 and the maximum is Rs. 5,00,00,000.
2.2 The maximum tenure of a home loan is 30 years.
2.3 The maximum loan to value ratio depends on the loan amount.
2.4 For loans up to Rs. 30 lakh, the maximum LTV is 90% of the property value.
2.5 For loans above Rs. 30 lakh and up to Rs. 75 lakh, the maximum LTV is 80% of the property value.
2.6 For loans above Rs. 75 lakh, the maximum LTV is 75% of the property value.

Section 3. Interest Rate and Fees
3.1 Home loans carry a floating interest rate linked to an external benchmark, the repo rate.
3.2 The spread over the benchmark ranges from 2.25% to 3.25% depending on the credit score and the loan amount.
3.3 The interest rate is reset every 3 months in line with changes in the benchmark.
3.4 A processing fee of 0.5% of the loan amount, subject to a maximum of Rs. 10,000, plus GST is charged.

Section 4. Disbursement
4.1 For a ready-to-move property, the loan is disbursed in full after the property registration.
4.2 For an under-construction property, the loan is disbursed in stages in line with the construction progress certified by the builder and the bank's engineer.
4.3 During staged disbursement, the borrower pays Pre-EMI, which is interest only on the amount disbursed so far, until the loan is fully disbursed.

Section 5. Prepayment and Foreclosure
5.1 No prepayment or foreclosure charges apply on floating rate home loans taken by individual borrowers.
5.2 Part prepayment is allowed at any time. The minimum part prepayment amount is Rs. 10,000.

Section 6. Documents and Insurance
6.1 KYC documents: PAN card and Aadhaar for all applicants.
6.2 Income documents for salaried applicants: salary slips for the last 3 months and bank statements for the last 6 months.
6.3 Property documents: sale agreement, title deed, approved building plan and a no objection certificate (NOC) from the builder or the housing society.
6.4 Property insurance is mandatory for the full tenure of the loan. Life insurance is optional.
6.5 Legal and technical valuation of the property is carried out by the bank's empanelled valuers, and the cost is borne by the applicant.
